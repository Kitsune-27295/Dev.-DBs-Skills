## Notes on disagreements and nuances across sources

- **Dependency direction at the bottom:** Sources 1, 3, and 4 note that in traditional Layered/N-Tier architecture, the Business Logic layer depends downward on the Data Access layer (`UI -> Business -> Data`). Source 4 explicitly contrasts this with Clean/Hexagonal Architecture, where Data Access is inverted to depend inward on the Business/Application core. This skill documents the traditional downward N-Layer rule and directs readers to `hexagonal-architecture` or `clean-architecture` when domain-centric dependency inversion is needed.
- **Strict vs. Relaxed layers:** Source 1 explicitly treats both strict (closed) and relaxed (open) communication models as valid contractual choices, noting that strict increases latency/indirection while relaxed increases coupling.
