## Notes on Claims & Nuances

- **Empirical/Practitioner Rules of Thumb (`*claim*`):** Richar​ds & Ford's First Law (*"Everything in software architecture is a trade-off"*), Ch. 18's *"synchronous by default, asynchronous when necessary"*, and Ted Neward's observation (*"architects only get fewer than a half-dozen greenfield architecture opportunities in their career"*) are practitioner heuristics (`*claim*`) rather than universal mathematical proofs.
- **Cloud Limits & Plans:** Specific Azure node minimums, instance scale caps, and service plan behaviors documented in Source 3 reflect Microsoft Learn documentation as of 2026 and must be verified against current cloud provider quotas for production sizing.
